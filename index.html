Research

Research agenda

My work asks how decision models can combine structural interpretability with strong out-of-sample performance, especially when data are limited, preferences are nonlinear, or risk and opportunity are asymmetric.

01

Decision Analytics under Risk

Theory and empirical methods for decisions under asymmetric downside risk and upside opportunity, including dominance-consistent rules and point-in-time financial evaluation.

02

Interpretable AI for MCDM

Constrained neural-network and GDT models that retain decision-theoretic structure while learning nonlinear relationships from data.

03

Reproducible Model Evaluation

Leakage-controlled out-of-sample validation, statistical inference, sensitivity analysis, and auditable computational workflows for managerial and engineering applications.
